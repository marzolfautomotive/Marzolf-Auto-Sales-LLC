Auto broker services

I'll help you find and purchase the right vehicle.

Not seeing what you want in the gallery? I can work with you to identify what you're looking for, discuss your budget and priorities, compare potential options, and coordinate next steps toward a purchase. Tell me what you need and we'll talk through the process.

1. Tell me what you want

Share your preferred make and model, budget, features, and how you'll use the vehicle.

2. Search and compare

Discuss potential vehicles and compare available information to help you make an informed decision.

3. Plan the purchase

Coordinate questions and purchase arrangements, with any fees and service terms discussed up front.

Broker services and arrangements are subject to applicable state licensing requirements and will be clarified before work begins.

Meet the owner

Wyatt Marzolf

Owner, Marzolf Auto Sales LLC

My name is Wyatt Marzolf, and my journey has not always been an easy one. For much of my life, I struggled with involvement in the criminal justice system and faced challenges that could have kept me from building the future I wanted.

Through difficult lessons, perseverance, and a determination to change my direction, I have worked to overcome trials and tribulations and build something positive for myself. My past is part of my story, but it does not define the person I am working to be or the future I am creating.

Starting Marzolf Auto Sales LLC represents progress, accountability, and the belief that people can learn from hardship and move forward. I want to build a business based on honest communication, treating people with respect, and helping customers navigate the process of finding and purchasing a vehicle.

I know what it means to face obstacles and keep going. My goal is to bring that same determination to my work and to earn each customer's trust one interaction at a time.
